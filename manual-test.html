Shape Creation Test

Instructions:
  1. Enter your Google Maps API key
  2. Click "Load Map & Polyfill"
  3. Use toolbar to select each shape type
  4. Draw the shape on the map
  5. Check off test when shape appears correctly

Test Checklist

1. Marker
Steps:
  • Click Marker tool (pin icon)
  • Click anywhere on map
  • Verify pin appears at click location
Marker works
2. Polyline
Steps:
  • Click Polyline tool (line icon)
  • Click 2+ points on map
  • Click white circle to finish
  • Verify line connects all points
Polyline works
3. Polygon
Steps:
  • Click Polygon tool
  • Click 3+ points on map
  • Click white circle at first point to close
  • Verify polygon closes and fills
Polygon works
4. Circle
Steps:
  • Click Circle tool
  • Click and drag to draw circle
  • Release mouse to finish
  • Verify circular shape appears
Circle works
5. Rectangle
Steps:
  • Click Rectangle tool
  • Click and drag to draw rectangle
  • Release mouse to finish
  • Verify rectangular shape appears
Rectangle works
6. Settings Restoration
Steps:
  • Draw a shape (any type)
  • Switch back to Pan (hand icon)
  • Verify map pans smoothly
  • Verify double-click zooms work
Settings restored
