Draw Order & Click-through Playground (TS-44301)

Its own map and manager. Tick Advanced markers before opening to get a 40 px custom finishing node (uses DEMO_MAP_ID).

Tools:
App content:
  • Draw on top: draw a polygon, then a circle and a polyline over it. Each new shape sits above the older ones; the log shows its zIndex (0, 1, 2…). A cancelled circle/rectangle (tiny drag) uses a number up.
  • Click-through: with a tool active, start a shape inside a green geofence or a shape you drew. It draws, and the log shows no geofence click. With Pan selected, geofences open their info window again.
  • Polyline taps: with DevTools touch emulation or on a tablet, tap 3–4 points. The line must not finish on the first or second tap; tapping the last point finishes it.
  • Polygon close: close a polygon on its first point. The log shows exactly the points placed (no spike or extra vertex). With Advanced markers, a click near the edge of the big node closes it too.
  • Custom popups: with a tool active, the purple popups (float pane and data-mcx-ui) log their own click and add no vertex. The red one has neither, so drawing through it is expected.
  • Controls: zoom, map type, the drawing toolbar and info window close buttons still work while a tool is active.
